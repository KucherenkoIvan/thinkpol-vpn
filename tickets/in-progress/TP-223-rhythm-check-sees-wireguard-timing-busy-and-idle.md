## Goal
M2's gate promises `reality` shows no WireGuard rhythm: no 2-minute rekey pair and no fixed-size keepalive records (docs/design/milestones.md, M2 row). Today the lab's rhythm signal only looks at records of 92–404 bytes (lab/censor/doc.go:169-170), so `reality`'s 512-byte floor passes it on size alone, and the matrix's hold pings every 2 s (lab/reality_matrix_test.go:86-87), so an idle tunnel is never tested. Make the check honest, then measure.

## Scope
- The rhythm detector flags WireGuard's rekey pair by timing, at any record size. Its thresholds come from WireGuard's own timers (REKEY_AFTER_TIME, KEEPALIVE_TIMEOUT, persistent keepalive), cited in lab/censor/doc.go, not tuned to any transport's result.
- An idle-tunnel case: the tunnel carries no application traffic for several rekey intervals.
- The reality × active-prober cell: today it accepts any text containing "to the reference during" (lab/results_test.go:547), and its comment admits an IPv6 link can report 0 of n (lab/results_test.go:541-543).

## Out of scope
- Changing any transport's shaping or timing (product code). If `reality` fails the honest check, this ticket stops at the measurement; the fix needs a design note the owner approves first (it would revisit RR65).

## Acceptance
- [x] The detector flags the rekey pair by timing at any record size when the pair stands out: it follows a quiet gap (REKEY_TIMEOUT, 5 s, with no record either way) or brings sizes new to the rekey period. Among steady records of its own size that signal is blind, and two signals that count records each way (count and burst, R2.1) see the rekey there (lab/censor/doc.go). Thresholds cited from WireGuard's timers in lab/censor/doc.go, the module's own choices (2 s answer window, 1 s steadiness, three occurrences, 300 ms count settle, 10 ms burst gap, three records a way in a burst) named as choices (R3.7).
- [x] Control: plain WireGuard (`udp`) is caught on a busy tunnel.
- [ ] Control: `udp` is caught on an idle tunnel. Not while thinkpol sets no persistent keepalive: an idle thinkpol tunnel sends no WireGuard rhythm (Notes). Open until TP-080 (liveness rides on data, idle path probes back off with jitter; owner, 2026-11-16) brings the idle case back.
- [ ] An idle-tunnel case exercises the keepalive claim. Built and measured (`rhythmCase(..., true)`, holdTunnel's idle branch; Notes), but out of the matrix until TP-080 brings it back (owner, 2026-11-16): thinkpol's own path probes are the idle tell.
- [ ] `reality`'s result on both cases measured and recorded in Notes: busy, caught by the record counts (burst and count, R2.1), a known tell RR65 accepts (owner, 2026-11-16), and the matrix expects that fail and its cell says so (R2.2) only while the module blocked it for the count or the burst (R3.1), shown failing as expected in the lab-gotest at LABSHA (Log); idle measured (Notes), the case out of the matrix.
- [ ] The reality × prober cell fails unless its verdict judges the tell: a dial to the camouflage target during every probe (count equals probes), or the tell reported unattributable when server-1 dials the target outside the probes at half their rate or more, which scores the cell `not judged`, never a pass (R3.2); IPv6's 0 of n is fixed (`hosts`).
- [ ] Each new or changed check mutation-checked; `make lint` and `make test` pass; the lab check is targeted runs, not a full `make lab-test` (the orchestrator's acceptance, 2026-11-16): `go test ./lab/censor/...` and a lab-gotest of the matrix's udp and reality rows × off, wireguard-rhythm and active prober, at LABSHA.
